**Language:** English

**Question:**
How do I book a medical exam through Mwfaq? (booking steps)

**Answer:**
In just three steps: 1) Choose your exam and appointment: pick the exam type, city, and date, then confirm your booking and pay securely via the platform or app. 2) Visit the center: go to the certified medical center at your scheduled time to complete the exam, and track your request status live via the platform. 3) Receive your certified report: as soon as your result is issued, you'll get a text message, and your certified medical report will be ready to download and share.
